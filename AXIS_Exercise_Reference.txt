THE BASELINE PROTOCOL (25 min)
25 exercises

Cat–Cow
Section: Warm-up & Mobility

  1. Neutral Tabletop
     Inhale. Let your belly drop toward the floor. Lift your gaze and tailbone gently.
  2. Cat, Exhale
     Exhale, round your spine toward the ceiling. Tuck your chin to your chest and pelvis under.
  3. Cow, Inhale
     Move with your breath. Don't force the lower back on the inhale.
  4. Finish
     Return to a neutral tabletop with your back flat. Repeat slow cycles.

Sphinx Pose
Section: Warm-up & Mobility

  1. Prone Start
     Bring your forearms to the floor with elbows directly under your shoulders.
  2. Lift
     Press forearms down to lift your chest. Keep hips on the floor. Hold for the full duration.
  3. Step 3
     This is a passive hold. Let the spine decompress. Don't force the lift.
  4. Finish
     Let your lower back relax. Do not push your chest too high.

Child's Pose
Section: Warm-up & Mobility

  1. Kneel Upright
     Fold forward and rest your forehead on the floor. Reach arms straight out in front.
  2. Fold Forward
     Breathe deeply into your lower back. Let your chest sink toward the floor. Hold.
  3. Step 3
     Widen your knees if you need more room for your torso.
  4. Finish
     Take deep breaths into your lower back. Hold.

Arm Circles (Forwards)
Section: Warm-up & Mobility

  1. Stand Tall
     Reach both arms straight out to your sides at shoulder height. Shoulders relaxed down.
  2. Forward Circles
     Both arms make small controlled circles moving forward. Gradually increase to full arm sweeps. Keep shoulders down.
  3. Full Range
     Continue forward circles with an easy rhythm. Slight squeeze of shoulder blades on the back phase.
  4. Finish
     Stay in the forward direction the whole time; increase range gradually.

Arm Circles (Backwards)
Section: Warm-up & Mobility

  1. Stand Tall
     Reach both arms straight out to your sides at shoulder height. Shoulders relaxed down.
  2. Backward Circles
     Both arms make small controlled circles moving backward. Gradually increase to full arm sweeps. Keep shoulders down.
  3. Full Range
     Continue backward circles with an easy rhythm. Keep shoulders away from your ears.
  4. Finish
     Stay in the backward direction the whole time; increase range gradually.

Pelvic Tilts
Section: Core & Posterior Chain

  1. Supine Neutral
     Gently tighten your abs and press your lower back flat into the floor. Hold 2 seconds.
  2. Posterior Tilt
     Relax and let the lower back arch slightly away from the floor. Then press flat again. Repeat slowly.
  3. Anterior Tilt
     The movement is small and subtle. Do not hold your breath.
  4. Finish
     Relax your stomach muscles and let your lower back lift again. Move slowly.

Glute Bridge
Section: Core & Posterior Chain

  1. Supine Setup
     Press through your heels and lift your hips until shoulders, hips, and knees form a straight line.
  2. Bridge Up
     Squeeze your glutes at the top. Hold briefly, then lower slowly back to the floor.
  3. Step 3
     Do not let your lower back arch at the top. Keep your core tight throughout.
  4. Finish
     Lower your hips slowly back to the floor.

Marching Bridge
Section: Core & Posterior Chain

  1. Hold Bridge
     Keeping hips level, slowly lift one foot off the floor and bring your knee to 90 degrees. Hold 2 seconds.
  2. Lift One Foot
     Lower that foot, then lift the other. Alternate slowly without letting your hips drop or rotate.
  3. Alternate
     Think of balancing a glass of water on your hips. Keep it level throughout.
  4. Finish
     Lower your right foot. Lift your left foot. Keep your hips completely still.

Dead Bug
Section: Core & Posterior Chain

  1. Supine Tabletop
     Slowly lower your right arm overhead and extend your left leg toward the floor simultaneously. Keep your lower back pressed into the floor.
  2. Extend Opposite Limbs
     Return to start. Repeat with your left arm and right leg. Move in slow 4-second counts each way.
  3. Return & Switch
     Opposite arm and leg always move together. Control matters more than range.
  4. Finish
     Bring your arm and leg back up. Switch sides. Move very slowly.

Bird Dog
Section: Core & Posterior Chain

  1. Neutral Tabletop
     Reach your right arm straight forward and extend your left leg straight back. Both parallel to the floor. Hold 2 seconds.
  2. Extend & Pause
     Draw elbow to knee beneath your body. Return to neutral. Switch sides and repeat.
  3. Return Controlled
     Opposite arm and leg, not the same side. Keep hips level and do not rotate.
  4. Finish
     Lower your hand and knee back to the floor. Switch sides.

High Plank
Section: Upper Body

  1. Hand Position
     Step feet back until your body forms one straight line from head to heels. Squeeze your glutes.
  2. Full Plank
     Pull your navel toward your spine and breathe steadily. Hold for the full duration.
  3. Step 3
     Do not let your hips sag or lift. If your wrists ache, drop to your forearms.
  4. Finish
     Squeeze your stomach and butt muscles. Do not let your hips sag down.

Wall Push-Ups
Section: Upper Body

  1. Wall Setup
     Bend your elbows and lower your chest toward the wall over 2 to 3 seconds. Keep your body in a straight line.
  2. Lower
     Push through your palms to return to start. Fully extend your arms. Pause briefly at the top.
  3. Press Back
     Step further from the wall to increase difficulty. No sagging hips.
  4. Finish
     Push the wall away to straighten your arms. Step your feet backward to make it harder.

Isometric Wall Push Hold
Section: Upper Body

  1. Wall Setup
     Bend your elbows to roughly 90 degrees. The midpoint of a wall push-up. Stop here.
  2. Halfway Hold
     Press your hands firmly into the wall and hold completely still for the full duration. Breathe steadily.
  3. Step 3
     This is a still exercise. No movement is the point. You will feel tension in your chest and arms.
  4. Finish
     Push your hands firmly into the wall. Keep your stomach muscles tight.

Resistance Band Row
Section: Upper Body

  1. Band Setup
     Sit tall with slight tension in the band at arm's length. Keep your back straight.
  2. Row Back
     Pull both hands toward your lower abdomen, driving elbows back and squeezing shoulder blades together. Pause 1 second, then extend slowly.
  3. Controlled Return
     Do not let your shoulders roll forward at full extension.
  4. Finish
     Straighten your arms slowly. Keep your back straight.

Wall Angels
Section: Upper Body

  1. Wall Contact
     Slowly slide your arms up the wall, maintaining contact at all times. Go only as far as contact allows.
  2. Slide Up
     Slide back down to start over 3 seconds. If contact is lost, reduce your range.
  3. Slide Down
     Quality over range. Back, head, and hands all stay on the wall throughout.
  4. Finish
     Slide your arms slowly back down. Stop if your arms pull away from the wall.

Supine Hamstring Stretch - Right Leg
Section: Lower Body & Rotation

  1. Supine Start
     Lift one leg, keeping the knee as straight as comfortable. Hold behind the thigh or calf with both hands. No reach? Use a towel around your foot.
  2. Raise One Leg
     Hold the stretch for the full duration. Feel the pull in the back of your thigh, not your knee. Switch sides when cued.
  3. Step 3
     Do not lock the knee. A mild pull is correct, sharp discomfort is not.
  4. Finish
     Pull your leg gently toward your chest until you feel a stretch. Keep your knee slightly bent.

Supine Hamstring Stretch - Left Leg
Section: Lower Body & Rotation

  1. Supine Start

  2. Raise One Leg

  3. Step 3

  4. Finish

Figure Four Stretch - Right
Section: Lower Body & Rotation

  1. Cross Leg Over Knee
     Lift your left foot off the floor and draw both legs toward your chest until you feel a stretch in your right hip.
  2. Draw In
     Hold for the full duration. Use your hand to gently press your right knee outward to deepen the stretch. Switch sides when cued.
  3. Step 3
     You should feel this in the outer hip and glute of the crossed leg.
  4. Finish
     Push your right knee gently away from your body to feel a deeper stretch.

Figure Four Stretch - Left
Section: Lower Body & Rotation

  1. Cross Leg Over Knee
     Lift your left foot off the floor and draw both legs toward your chest until you feel a stretch in your right hip.
  2. Draw In
     Hold for the full duration. Use your hand to gently press your right knee outward to deepen the stretch. Switch sides when cued.
  3. Step 3

Windshield Wipers
Section: Lower Body & Rotation

  1. Supine, Feet Flat
     Keeping knees together, slowly lower both knees to the right as far as is comfortable. Keep both shoulders on the floor.
  2. Lower Knees Right
     Sweep knees back through center and lower to the left. Move at a slow windshield-wiper pace. Continue alternating.
  3. Lower Knees Left
     For an easier version, keep feet on the floor and let knees drop side to side from there.
  4. Finish
     Lift your knees back to the middle, then let them drop slowly to the left side.

Reclined Spinal Twist - Right Side
Section: Lower Body & Rotation

  1. Supine Start
     Guide that knee across your body with the opposite hand. Let it fall toward the floor.
  2. Cross & Hold
     Turn your gaze in the opposite direction if comfortable. Hold for the full duration. Switch sides when cued.
  3. Step 3
     Both shoulders stay on the floor. Let gravity do the work. Do not force the knee down.
  4. Finish
     Keep your right shoulder flat on the floor. Let gravity pull your knee down.

Reclined Spinal Twist - Left Side
Section: Lower Body & Rotation

  1. Supine Start
     Guide that knee across your body with the opposite hand. Let it fall toward the floor.
  2. Cross & Hold
     Turn your gaze in the opposite direction if comfortable. Hold for the full duration. Switch sides when cued.
  3. Step 3
     Both shoulders stay on the floor. Let gravity do the work. Do not force the knee down.
  4. Finish
     Keep your left shoulder flat on the floor. Let gravity pull your knee down.

Seated Forward Fold
Section: Finish

  1. Seated Tall
     Inhale to lengthen your spine. Hinge forward from your hips, not your waist, and walk your hands toward your feet.
  2. Fold Forward
     Let your spine round naturally as you settle into the stretch. Head heavy. Hold for the full duration.
  3. Step 3
     A long spine matters more than reaching your toes. Bend your knees slightly if your hamstrings are tight.
  4. Finish
     Let your head hang heavy. Do not force yourself to reach your toes.

Chin Tucks
Section: Finish

  1. Neutral Head
     Draw your chin straight back, not down, as if making a double chin. The movement is purely horizontal.
  2. Retract
     Hold for 2 seconds. Release fully. Repeat for the full duration.
  3. Step 3
     You should feel a gentle stretch at the base of your skull. No tilting up or down.
  4. Finish
     Keep your head level. Do not point your chin up or down.

Neck Rolls
Section: Finish

  1. Neutral Start
     Drop your right ear toward your right shoulder. Keep your left shoulder down. Hold 2 to 3 seconds. Return through center.
  2. Right Tilt
     Drop your left ear toward your left shoulder. Smooth arc only, ear to ear through the front. Never through the back.
  3. Left Tilt
     Do not roll the head backward. Keep the movement slow and controlled throughout.
  4. Finish
     Roll your chin slowly down to your chest, then lift your left ear toward your left shoulder.

LYMPH & FLOW (15 min)
15 exercises

Lymph Hops
Section: Full Reset

  1. Soft Stand
     Feet hip-width. Knees soft, never locked. Arms relaxed at sides. Jaw loose. Breathe naturally.
  2. Micro Bounce
     Rise gently onto the balls of your feet and let your heels drop back down. Barely leave the ground. Think: soft vibration, not jumps.
  3. Rhythm
     Find a relaxed 1–2 beat. Let arms dangle and bounce passively. Face and shoulders stay completely soft. This should feel effortless.
  4. Finish
     Find a slow rhythm. Keep your face and shoulders soft.

Body Waves
Section: Full Reset

  1. Stand Loose
     Feet hip-width, knees slightly bent. Arms at sides. Imagine your spine is a string of loose beads.
  2. Initiate Wave
     Begin the wave at the top: let your head nod gently forward, then ripple the movement down through neck, chest, belly.
  3. Full Undulation
     As the wave reaches the pelvis, let it curl forward then rise back up. Continuous, fluid figure-8. No forcing. Gravity does the work.
  4. Finish
     When the wave reaches your hips, curl them forward and rise back up.

Arm Swings
Section: Full Reset

  1. Loose Stance
     Feet slightly wider than hips. Knees soft. Let arms hang completely relaxed from the shoulders.
  2. Initiate Swing
     Turn torso gently left. Arms swing right passively, carried by momentum. Do not muscle the arms. They follow the body.
  3. Continuous Turn
     Turn right. Arms swing left. Find a pendulum rhythm. Arms may tap your sides lightly. Increase range gradually as you warm up.
  4. Finish
     Turn your chest to the right. Let your arms swing to the left. Find a steady rhythm.

Forward / Backward Arm Circles
Section: Full Reset

  1. Stand Tall
     Feet hip-width. Arms at sides. Shoulders relaxed down. Begin with small circles and gradually increase the range.
  2. Forward Circles
     Both arms circle forward simultaneously. 15 slow rotations. Feel the shoulder joint moving through its full range.
  3. Backward Circles
     Reverse direction. 15 slow rotations backward. Backward circles open the chest and counteract forward shoulder posture.
  4. Finish
     Switch directions. Move both arms in circles backward 15 times.

Dead Arms
Section: Full Reset

  1. Slight Forward Lean
     Hinge slightly at the hips. Let both arms hang completely dead. No muscle engagement. Jaw loose.
  2. Gentle Sway
     Let the arms sway passively as you shift your weight side to side. Do not initiate any movement with the shoulder muscles. Gravity only.
  3. Small Circles
     Let the arms trace small passive circles, clockwise, then counter-clockwise. This gently tractions the shoulder joints.
  4. Finish
     Let your arms move in small circles. Keep your shoulders relaxed.

Bent Over Back Shakes
Section: Full Reset

  1. Hinge Forward
     Hinge at the hips, knees soft. Let the upper body hang forward. Arms dangling, head heavy. Let gravity decompress the spine.
  2. Gentle Shake
     Softly shake the upper body, small, effortless oscillations. Let the spine wobble freely. This releases the erectors and multifidus.
  3. Side Sway
     Add a gentle side-to-side sway. Let the arms swing. Stay loose. This is pure release. Nothing to engage.
  4. Finish
     Keep your arms loose. Do not use any muscles to hold yourself up.

Trunk Twists
Section: Full Reset

  1. Loose Stance
     Feet shoulder-width, knees soft. Arms hang at sides. Spine tall but not stiff.
  2. Twist Right
     Rotate torso to the right. Arms swing passively. Left arm wraps across the front, right swings behind. Pivot on left ball of foot.
  3. Twist Left
     Swing back through center and rotate left. Right arm wraps front, left swings behind. Find a loose, rhythmic pendulum pace.
  4. Finish
     Rotate back through center, then turn your chest to the left. Let your right arm wrap across your front and your left arm swing behind. Alternate sides in a steady rhythm.

Golf Swings
Section: Full Reset

  1. Address Position
     Feet shoulder-width. Slight hip hinge. Arms hang loose. Imagine holding a club, but keep the grip soft.
  2. Backswing
     Rotate through the hips and torso to the right, letting the arms follow passively. Left heel can lift slightly. No forcing.
  3. Through Swing
     Swing through to the left in one fluid motion. Arms follow the torso. Do not lead with the arms. Alternate sides for 1 minute.
  4. Finish
     Swing your arms to the left in one smooth motion. Do not force the movement.

Twist the Waist
Section: Full Reset

  1. Stand Tall
     Feet hip-width. Hands on hips. Eyes forward. Upper body completely still throughout, only the hips move.
  2. Hip Rotation
     Rotate the pelvis to the right, then sweep left in a continuous figure-8 or side-to-side motion. Upper body stays square.
  3. Find Rhythm
     Increase the range gradually. Feel the lumbar spine rotating freely. This is the movement that desk work robs you of most.
  4. Finish
     Make the circles bigger as you go. Move only your hips.

March Taps
Section: Full Reset

  1. Upright Stance
     Stand tall. Shoulders relaxed. This is a gentle march, not a high-knee drill. Pace is easy and rhythmic.
  2. Lift & Tap
     Lift right knee to comfortable height, tap with left hand. Lower. Lift left knee, tap with right hand. Alternate continuously.
  3. Add Arm Swing
     Let the opposite arm swing naturally with each step, just like walking. This cross-body pattern activates lymphatic flow bilaterally.
  4. Finish
     Let your other arm swing naturally, like you are walking.

Wide Arm Step Backs
Section: Full Reset

  1. Neutral Start
     Feet together, arms at sides. Soft knees. This movement is slow and deliberate, not aerobic.
  2. Step & Open
     Step right foot back and wide as both arms sweep out to the sides and overhead, like opening a large book. Inhale.
  3. Return & Close
     Step feet back together, arms return to sides. Exhale. Alternate sides. The arm sweep opens the chest and axillary lymph nodes.
  4. Finish
     Step your feet back together and lower your arms. Switch to the left foot.

Ballet Squats
Section: Full Reset

  1. Wide Stance
     Feet wider than hips, toes turned out to roughly 10 and 2 o'clock. Arms out at sides or hands at heart center.
  2. Plié Down
     Bend knees, tracking them over toes. Lower slowly, 3 seconds down. Keep torso upright, tailbone dropping straight down.
  3. Rise Up
     Press through the full foot to return to standing, 2 seconds up. Gently squeeze inner thighs at the top. Do not lock knees.
  4. Finish
     Press your feet into the floor to stand back up for 2 seconds. Keep your knees slightly bent at the top.

Standing Calf Raise
Section: Full Reset

  1. Flat Foot
     Feet hip-width. Arms relaxed at sides. Soft knees. This is the low point of the movement.
  2. Rise & Reach
     Rise onto tiptoes as arms sweep upward and forward to shoulder height or overhead. Inhale on the rise.
  3. Lower & Swing
     Lower heels as arms swing back down and behind. Exhale. The arm swing is passive. Gravity-assisted. Continuous and flowing.
  4. Finish
     Lower your heels to the floor. Let your arms swing down and behind you.

Doorway Chest Stretch
Section: Full Reset

  1. Doorway Position
     Stand in a doorway. Place both forearms on the frame at 90°, elbows at shoulder height, upper arms parallel to floor.
  2. Step Through
     Step one foot forward through the doorway. Let your body lean gently into the opening. Feel the chest and front shoulders open.
  3. Hold & Breathe
     Hold without forcing. Take 3 slow, deep breaths. Each exhale, let the chest open a little more. Do not shrug shoulders.
  4. Finish
     Take 3 slow breaths. Let your chest drop forward a little more each time you breathe out.

Wall Pushups
Section: Full Reset

  1. Hands on Wall
     Stand an arm's length from the wall. Place hands at shoulder height, slightly wider than shoulder-width. Body in a straight line.
  2. Lower In
     Bend elbows and lean chest toward the wall. Keep the body rigid. No sagging hips. Elbows track at roughly 45° from the torso.
  3. Press Out
     Push back to start. Exhale on the push. Move slowly and with control. This is not about speed. It's about reactivating the chest and shoulders.
  4. Finish
     Push the wall away to straighten your arms. Move slowly.

THE FOUNDATION RESET (~22 min)
10 exercises

Pelvic Tilts
Section: Foundation sequence

  1. Supine Neutral
     Gently tighten your abs and press your lower back flat into the floor. Hold 2 seconds.
  2. Posterior Tilt
     Relax and let the lower back arch slightly away from the floor. Then press flat again. Repeat slowly.
  3. Anterior Tilt
     The movement is small and subtle. Do not hold your breath.
  4. Finish
     Relax your stomach muscles and let your lower back lift again. Move slowly.

Chin Tucks
Section: Foundation sequence

  1. Neutral Head
     Draw your chin straight back, not down, as if making a double chin. The movement is purely horizontal.
  2. Retract
     Hold for 2 seconds. Release fully. Complete 10 smooth reps. Stop if you feel dizziness or sharp neck pain.
  3. Step 3
     You should feel a gentle stretch at the base of your skull. No tilting up or down.
  4. Finish
     Keep your head level. Do not point your chin up or down.

Modified Bird-Dog
Section: Foundation sequence

  1. 1/4
     Start on your hands and knees on the floor.
  2. 2/4
     Slowly extend one leg straight back behind you.
  3. 3/4
     Keep your lower back still and your pelvis parallel to the ground.
  4. 4/4
     Lower your leg and repeat the movement with the other side.

Wall Slides (W to Y)
Section: Foundation sequence

  1. Wall Contact
     Stand with head, upper back, and glutes touching the wall. Arms in a W shape, elbows bent, backs of hands against the wall. This is your baseline.
  2. Slide to Y
     Keeping the backs of hands and elbows touching the wall, slowly slide arms upward until they form a Y overhead. If contact is lost, reduce range. The lower trapezius must fire to keep the shoulder blades down and in.
  3. Slow Return
     Lower arms back to W in 3 seconds. Do not shrug or let shoulders elevate. 12 reps. This directly reverses the upper trap dominance that carrying luggage creates.
  4. Finish
     Slide your arms back down to a W shape. Do 12 slow reps.

Box Squats
Section: Foundation sequence

  1. Setup
     Feet hip-width, toes forward. Brace your core. Imagine sitting into a tall chair behind you.
  2. Sit Back
     Send hips back first. Lower until you graze the box or chair without collapsing. Knees track over toes.
  3. Stand
     Press evenly through mid-foot and heel to stand. Stop if knees or sharp back pain appears. Reduce depth or skip.
  4. Finish
     Move slowly. If balance feels unsure, use a higher surface or hand support on a wall.

Wall Sit
Section: Foundation sequence

  1. Wall Contact
     Stand with back flat against a wall. Feet shoulder-width, about 12 inches in front.
  2. Slide Down
     Slide down into a partial squat. Hold. Isometric holds have a pain-numbing effect on joints.
  3. Breathe
     Hold 45 seconds. Breathe normally.
  4. Finish
     Keep your back flat against the wall. Hold for 45 seconds.

Dead Bug
Section: Foundation sequence

  2. Extend Opposite Limbs

  3. Return & Switch

  4. Finish
     Bring your arm and leg back up. Switch sides. Move very slowly.

Forearm Plank
Section: Foundation sequence

  1. Forearm Setup
     Elbows under shoulders, forearms parallel. Tuck toes and lift knees so the body forms one line from head to heels.
  2. Brace
     Pull belly button up, squeeze glutes gently, breathe in the ribs. Do not hold breath.
  3. Step 3
     If form breaks or you feel sharp low-back pain, lower knees to the floor and hold a shorter lever, or end the set.
  4. Finish
     Drop to knees for a shorter hold if needed. Quality over duration.

NIGHT EASE (10 min)
6 exercises

Legs Up the Wall
Section: Better Sleep Stretch

  1. Setup
     Sit sideways against the wall, then swing legs up. Hips as close to the wall as comfortable. Arms out at sides, palms up.
  2. Hold & Breathe
     Let gravity drain the legs. Close the eyes. Breathe naturally. This posture reverses venous pooling and calms the nervous system.
  3. Step 3
     Place a folded blanket under your hips if your lower back is tight. Stay as long as feels good.
  4. Finish
     Place a folded blanket under your hips if your lower back feels tight.

Reclined Butterfly
Section: Better Sleep Stretch

  1. Soles Together
     Lie on back. Bring the soles of your feet together and let knees fall wide. Place hands on belly or out at sides.
  2. Gravity Hold
     Let the inner thighs and groin soften completely. Do not push. Hold for the full duration with easy breathing.
  3. Step 3
     Support the outer thighs with pillows if the stretch feels too intense.
  4. Finish
     Place pillows under your knees if the stretch is too intense.

Knees to Chest Rock
Section: Better Sleep Stretch

  1. Hug Knees
     Lie on back. Hug both knees to chest. Interlock fingers over shins.
  2. Rock Side to Side
     Rock gently left and right. Let the lower back release against the floor. 10–15 slow rocks.
  3. Step 3
     Move slowly. This is a massage for your lower back. Let the floor do the work.
  4. Finish
     Move slowly. Let the floor massage your back.

Supine Twist - Right Side
Section: Better Sleep Stretch

  1. Setup
     Lie on back. Draw right knee to chest. Arms in a T, shoulders grounded.
  2. Cross Right
     Guide right knee across to the left with left hand. Turn gaze right. Hold for 45–60s.
  3. Cross Left
     Return through center. Draw left knee across right. Gaze left. Hold another 45–60s.
  4. Finish
     Bring your right knee back to the middle. Pull your left knee across to the right side. Hold.

Supine Twist - Left Side
Section: Better Sleep Stretch

  1. Setup
     Lie on back. Draw right knee to chest. Arms in a T, shoulders grounded.
  2. Cross Right
     Guide right knee across to the left with left hand. Turn gaze right. Hold for 45–60s.
  3. Cross Left
     Return through center. Draw left knee across right. Gaze left. Hold another 45–60s.
  4. Finish
     Bring your right knee back to the middle. Pull your left knee across to the right side. Hold.

Diaphragmatic Breathing
Section: Better Sleep Stretch

  1. Comfortable Position
     Lie flat or sit upright. Eyes closed. Place one hand on the chest, one on the belly.
  2. Inhale 4
     Breathe in through the nose for 4 slow counts. Feel the belly rise before the chest.
  3. Hold 7, Exhale 8
     Hold for 7 counts. Exhale completely through the mouth for 8 counts. Repeat 4–6 cycles.
  4. Finish
     If holding breath after inhale feels uncomfortable, skip the hold and use a simple 4-count in, 6-count out instead.
